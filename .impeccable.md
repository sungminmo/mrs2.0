## Design Context

### Users
Construction-company operations staff use the PC dashboard to manage recovered materials, inventory, sales listings, purchases, and transaction status efficiently.

### Brand Personality
Trustworthy, sustainable, and business-focused. The interface should feel calm, precise, and credible rather than consumer-retail oriented.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light, operational B2B workspace with forest-green brand accents, restrained surfaces, and information-dense desktop layouts. The EcoMatX material marketplace concept is the visual reference.

### Design Principles
- Prioritize scanability for repeated operational tasks.
- Use color purposefully for status, value, and primary actions.
- Keep navigation and controls predictable and keyboard-accessible.
- Maintain sufficient contrast and honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Prefer compact, durable information layouts over decorative marketing treatments.
